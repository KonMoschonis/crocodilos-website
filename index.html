The story

Born in a puddle.
Raised on hops.

Crocodilos opened its doors in Toumpa with one mission: give Thessaloniki a beer bar that takes its beer seriously and nothing else too seriously. No dress code, no attitude — just cold taps, a bottleshop deep enough to get lost in, and a house crocodile who silently judges your order from the wall. Sample
