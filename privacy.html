Privacy

Privacy Notice

This site is simple by design: no accounts, no online orders, no forms, and no analytics or advertising trackers of our own.

Third-party services we use

A couple of pieces of this site are loaded from Google, which means your browser connects directly to Google's servers when you visit:

  • Google Fonts — used for the typefaces on every page.
  • Google Maps — embedded on the homepage to show our location.

Loading these can share basic technical information (like your IP address) with Google. We don't control what Google does with that — see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

What we don't do

We don't use cookies of our own, don't run analytics or ad-tracking scripts, don't collect personal data through forms, and don't sell or share any information — because we don't have any to begin with.
